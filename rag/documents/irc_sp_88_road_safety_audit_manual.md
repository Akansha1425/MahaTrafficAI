# IRC SP:88 Road Safety Audit Manual — Summary

**Issuing Body:** Indian Roads Congress (IRC)  
**Document:** IRC:SP:88-2019 "Guidelines for Road Safety Audit"  
**Applicability:** All national and state highways in India, including Maharashtra

---

## 1. Purpose of Road Safety Audit

A Road Safety Audit (RSA) is a formal examination of an existing or future road project to determine its potential to cause road accidents or casualties. The audit independently evaluates road safety performance using a defined checklist methodology.

---

## 2. Black Spot Identification Method (IRC)

IRC recommends the following quantitative method for black spot identification:
- **Accident Frequency Method:** Sections with 5+ injury accidents in 3 years qualify as black spots.
- **Accident Rate Method:** Accident rate per vehicle-kilometer-traveled (VKT) above threshold triggers black spot classification.
- **Severity Index:** Weighted score = (12 × Fatal + 3 × Grievous + 1 × Simple) / Total accidents. Sites scoring > 15 are high priority.

---

## 3. Road Safety Inspection Checklist (Key Items)

### 3.1 Geometry and Alignment
- Adequate sight distance at intersections and curves (min 180m stopping sight distance on highways).
- Transition curves at sharp bends.
- Adequate shoulder width (minimum 2.5m on 2-lane highways).

### 3.2 Road Furniture
- Chevron signs at curves exceeding 30° deflection.
- Delineators at 10m spacing in curves.
- W-beam guardrails on embankments > 3m height.

### 3.3 Junctions
- Priority control signs at all intersections.
- Channelization islands for turning movements.
- Adequate sight lines cleared of vegetation.

### 3.4 Lighting
- Continuous street lighting at junctions, school zones, and pedestrian crossings.
- Minimum 30 lux illuminance on arterial roads.

### 3.5 Pedestrian Facilities
- Footpath continuity audit.
- Zebra crossings with tactile paving at 50m intervals in urban zones.
- At-grade crossings with 2-minute gap acceptance.

## 4. Key Risk Factors Identified in Maharashtra Audits

Based on road safety audits conducted on NH-48 (Mumbai-Pune), NH-60, and SH-27 (Ahmednagar-Pune):

1. **Missing crash barriers** on high embankment sections in Ghat zones.
2. **Inadequate junction illumination** on rural NH sections.
3. **Poor delineation** on curves at night (reflective markers missing or faded).
4. **Encroachment** of road shoulders by informal market stalls.
5. **Absence of speed table/humps** near school/hospital zones in small towns.
6. **Drains blocked** leading to ponding and skidding in monsoon.
