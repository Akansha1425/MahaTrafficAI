ℹ️ Understanding District Composite Risk Scores:
District rankings are determined by the 4-factor composite formula: 0.40×Severity + 0.25×Frequency + 0.20×RoadFactor + 0.15×TimeFactor (0–100 scale) over the 2019–2023 period. Because Mumbai has the highest total accident frequency in Maharashtra (28,372 accidents = maximum frequency score 100), its volume score outweighs Pune's per-accident severity lead, resulting in a composite score of 45.07 vs Pune's 43.31.

🛡️ Multi-Layer Guardrail Architecture

1. Input Guardrails: Regex & token matching reject prompt injections, jailbreaks, financial spam, and out-of-domain requests before reaching planner.

2. Tool Schema Guardrails: MCP tools validate required fields and bounds (e.g. fatal_accidents ≤ accident_count).

3. Output Guardrails: ReviewerAgent validates that generated responses contain mandatory historical data disclaimers and do not fabricate real-time emergency guidance.

🔬 Methodological Limitations & Empirical Evaluation Notes:
  • Model Target Derivation: The ML target (risk_category) is rule-derived from record_risk_score using features like deaths and fatal_accidents. Consequently, the model classifies historical risk tiers rather than predicting independent future accident occurrences.
  • Adversarial Benchmark Scope: The 40-case synthetic benchmark demonstrates strong resistance (85.0%) to common attack templates, but cannot guarantee full immunity against novel or unobserved adversarial strategies.
  • RAG Benchmark Size: Groundedness was evaluated on a curated benchmark of 6 core road-safety questions against MoRTH and IRC guidelines.
